- **Summary:** Municipal-regulation radar for multi-site operators and the trade associations that represent them. Federal and state legislative tracking is a mature enterprise category (FiscalNote, Quorum, LegiScan-style feeds, ~$10k+/yr), but nobody covers the ~19,000 US municipalities where the rules that actually bite a 20-store franchisee get passed — third-party delivery fee caps, drive-thru and idling bans, single-use packaging rules, predictive scheduling, sign and awning codes, short-term-rental and storage-ordinance changes. Gavelwatch ingests city council agendas/minutes/ordinances nightly, ranks each item for relevance to a given member's store list, and sends a plain-English alert with the hearing date, the comment deadline, the money at stake and a one-page position memo. Weekly, it shows which rules are spreading city to city.
- **Why it's novel:** The structural reason nobody has done this is economics: enterprise gov-affairs tools cover federal + 50 states because that is a bounded number of feeds, while municipal coverage is fragmented across thousands of portals, PDF agendas and CMSes — until LLMs, reading and relevance-ranking that volume was not affordable.
